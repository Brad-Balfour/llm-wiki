# Instinct

Instinct is a useful case for assistants moving from private tasks into shared
human workflows. The October 6 commute requested one combined account of its
September and October announcements, original links, architectural questions,
and relevance to Range.

The directory describes September 9 coordination among users' Instincts in a
trusted circle. Its September 23 entry describes encrypted PDFs, images, and
spreadsheets delivered into recipients' workspaces, plus invitation links still
requiring approval. These are reported product claims, not a security audit.
TechCrunch reports group agents, revocable trust, and membership-aware replies.
No source here establishes a first-ever agent-to-agent protocol.

## Architecture Questions and Commute Synthesis

Distinguish the personal assistant, shared group agent, and cross-user exchange
as possible principals. Their actual identity, authentication, state, execution,
and protocol boundaries remain undocumented in the evidence read here.

Trusting a person or group is different from authorizing a particular disclosure,
tool call, purchase, or commitment. What is the scope and duration of consent,
who grants it, and what durable record proves it? What happens to queued replies
or delegated capabilities when trust is revoked or group membership changes?
Already delivered files and learned context may have different recall limits.

What enforces isolation among users and personal/group contexts: credentials,
logs, caches, memory, file access, and execution identities? Encrypted transfer
alone does not prove those boundaries. How are invitations, non-account
participants, and cross-provider agents authenticated and governed?

For Range, the commute-derived product relevance is participation in shared
workflows, permissioned context sharing, explicit commitment authority, durable
ownership, revocation, and membership-aware disclosure. This is an evaluation
lens, not a claim about Range's implementation or an instruction to copy Instinct.

of this item; no numeric score is inferred.
