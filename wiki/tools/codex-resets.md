# Codex Resets

Codex Resets is an unofficial tracker that collects public posts about Codex
usage-limit resets and related service incidents. It is useful as a lightweight
historical signal when a change in Codex availability or limits needs context.

## Key Ideas

- The tracker is a convenience view of public announcements, not an official
  status service.
- Its timeline makes recurring resets and incident communications easier to
  compare over time.
- For a current operational decision, verify against OpenAI's official status
  and product communications as well.
