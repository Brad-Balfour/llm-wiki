# Natural Direct

Natural's [announcement](https://www.natural.com/blog/direct), by Krish
Dholakiya, presents Direct as its generally available payment product for
agents. It describes choosing among ACH, domestic wire, and real-time payment
rails including RTP and FedNow; checks and international wires were planned.
The queue's author lookup had failed; the byline was verified on the source.

The commute discussion asked whether this is also a brokerage or custodian
integration. Payment rails move cash; a receiving custodian may permit an ACH
or wire to fund an account, but that possibility does not establish a dedicated
brokerage integration. ACATS asset transfers are a separate workflow. The
announcement does not document ACATS, Apex, Altruist, or custodian integrations.
The discussion reported no such mention in the product and developer material
it inspected; that limited search does not rule out other documentation or
undisclosed arrangements.
