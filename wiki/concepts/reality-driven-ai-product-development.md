# Reality-Driven AI Product Development

AI can make prototyping and implementation much faster, but it does not replace direct user contact, market feedback, or testing a product's assumptions. AI-assisted building becomes counterproductive when polished output substitutes for those reality checks.

## Key Ideas

- Faster implementation shifts more of the work toward choosing worthwhile problems and validating solutions.
- Direct conversations with users and repeated feedback help prevent AI-assisted building from becoming avoidance.
- Coding speed, landing-page copy, and presentations may be useful, but they are not by themselves evidence that a product solves a real problem.
