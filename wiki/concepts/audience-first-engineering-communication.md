# Audience-First Engineering Communication

Start an announcement by naming the people affected so everyone else can move on. A summary tells readers what changed. An audience line helps them decide whether they need to read the summary. The commute discussion asked whether this extends from blog posts and email to team chat, and what a concrete example looks like.

## Source Notes

### [Assume People Are Not Interested in Your Blog Post](https://danielsada.tech/blog/assume-people-are-not-interested-in-your-blog-post/)

<!-- source-item-id: 20261123-tldr-dev:dev-003 -->

Daniel Sada recalls emails at Snowflake that opened by naming the relevant team or part of the codebase. He suggests a three-to-five-line introduction in Slack or Teams, with details in the thread. His example directs developers of the Java codebase to a new logger and leaves implementation and migration details in the thread.
