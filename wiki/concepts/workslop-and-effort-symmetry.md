# Workslop and Effort Symmetry

Workslop is AI-generated communication that is cheap to produce but expensive
for someone else to interpret. The useful boundary is not whether AI touched
the message. It is whether the sender invested enough judgment to make the
receiver's effort proportionate to the value conveyed.

## Key Ideas

- AI can make the cost of producing plausible text approach zero while leaving
  the recipient's reading, verification, and response costs intact.
- Thoughtful AI-assisted writing is not automatically workslop. The problem is
  the transfer of unfiltered processing work to another person.
- The direct response is to ask a sender to stop when authority and trust make
  that practical.
- Other defenses restore symmetry: summarize incoming text with AI, move the
  exchange to a call, or give low-effort material only low-effort attention.
- Durable artifacts may serve audit, reference, or compliance purposes beyond
  immediate communication, so apparent unreadability is not always evidence
  that the artifact has no value.

## The Asymmetry

Sean Goedecke compares workslop to a denial-of-service pattern: a sender can
generate a large volume in seconds, while a recipient must spend real time
finding the claims, checking them, and deciding what deserves a response. That
asymmetry makes volume a poor proxy for care or value.

The distinction matters because AI style alone is not diagnostic. A person may
use a model and still revise, verify, and structure the result carefully. In
that case the human has paid the judgment cost before transmission. Workslop
instead leaves that judgment to the receiver.

## Practical Defenses

The source offers a graduated response:

1. Ask the sender to stop when the relationship permits a direct correction.
2. Treat a person who merely relays model output as a slow interface to the
   model and steer the exchange accordingly.
3. Use another model to extract salient points or draft a proportionate reply.
4. Move to a call or in-person conversation, where both sides must spend time
   and ambiguity is exposed quickly.
5. Skim, defer, or ignore low-stakes output whose sender invested little effort.

The commute discussion emphasized these practical protections. They are not a
license to dismiss every long document. The operating test is whether the
format, stakes, and sender effort justify the attention being requested.
